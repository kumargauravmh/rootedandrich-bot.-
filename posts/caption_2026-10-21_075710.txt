1. You stop waiting for a divine sign to start saving that first $5,000; just open the high-yield account and move the damn money.
2. You realize that praying for a promotion doesn't replace the 80 hours of actual, painful work you aren't doing yet.
3. You cut the subscription services you don't even use—that $150 a month is enough for a serious book habit or an extra principal payment.
4. You stop apologizing for wanting to build wealth; money is just a tool, and you're currently choosing to keep your toolset empty.
5. You understand that faith is about how you handle the losses when your portfolio dips, not just how you act when you're winning.
