Ben Franklin retired at 42. Not by luck. He ran a tracking system on himself: 13 virtues, one per week, a black spot for every fault, every night.

He failed at some of it. He admitted it. And still said the attempt made him a better and a happier man.

What's the one habit you'd track for 13 weeks?

Source: Franklin's Autobiography (ch. IX) and Harvard Magazine.
